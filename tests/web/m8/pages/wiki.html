A screen reader is a form of assistive technology that renders text and image content as speech or braille output.[1] Screen readers are essential to people who are blind.[2]

History [edit]

Early years [edit]

The first screen readers ran on text-mode terminals. Rust-era tools came much later.[3]

Modern era [edit]

Modern readers build a virtual buffer of the page. They offer these commands:

  • Quick navigation by heading
  • Quick navigation by landmark
  • A list of links, headings and form fields
